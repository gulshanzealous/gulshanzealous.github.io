I have a brain which has found solace in nothingness. I measure my self worth by gauging the effort I have been able to get out of me every minute I realise my consciousness. The feedback loop is negative because the burden of backlog and unfulfilled works haunts and stops me from doing what I must do. With tens of incomplete works started on a whim and forgotten about because they are wiped out because the meaning associated with them fades away. I get up everyday feeling purposeless and the haunted by the past. Even the pleasures I try to indulge in to clear my brain add to my regret log and this vicious cycle cripples me to a point where I don't get anything other than bare minimum done. The rut I have been facing is killing my desire to live. I have goals I want to achieve and I am failing to drive myself towards them day to day because my confidence, sense of purpose, sense of belonging, burden of incumbent responsibilites stray me and I see the days, months, years pass by. My hair whitening and my brain slowing tell me that my time is running out and in the panic, I try to escape by shutting down my consciousness - by sleeping at odd hours. I feel like a coward running away from what I want to do but what I want to do has become so overwhelming for me that my brain and my resolve are fading due to the absence of any positivity. The direction of the world and general disarray make me more and more despondent. I am tired of the rut and want to get out of the jail I have build myself.

HabitRabbit is a small step in that direction. Write down a small list of tasks with small achievable goals which you want to do regularly and that small win will help in scaling the bigger things. It's like placing a single brick on a wall everyday until the wall is high enough for you to hold your head high. May I get there and see you there with a smile.
