Curiosity leads us to the unknown and uncovers new facts that help us navigate the boredom. However, there is something else that has a relationship to the curiosity - hope. The hope that the next time I will go down this road of uninhibited exploration, I will change the status quo for the better. 

I feel that I still have a lot of curiosity within me to solve problems in the world and repay the debt of oxygen I consume. However, my efforts are dimmed by the lack of hope. The dispair that my efforts to a certain goal are either incorrect or insufficient and I won't be able to bring myself to bridge that gap due to the scar tissue of countless failures and the torture I feel remembering them. The consciousness of my head berates me for my inaction and slow progress. My fear of not being able to fulfil my own minimum expectations off me poison my soul every moment. The torment is unending and leads to crippling procrastination.

I ventured into programming because I liked the idea of starting a blank canvas and being able to decide what I want to build. Bills and solitude have led me to do the work in the jobs which limit what I want to learn to become the engineer I envisioned so long ago. Infact, I have hated doing it and not being able to do it with sustained passion. Doing it to get it done and have a fleeting moment of diminishing gratitude becomes more and more unsustainable.

I need to find a way to feel better.
